# Bio EN — short & long

## Short version (≈ 130 words)

Brazilian multi-percussionist originally from São Vicente and now based in Belgium, **Nyllo Canella** is the creator of **A SELVA**, a live Samba Tech project where Afro-Brazilian percussion — samba, afoxé, samba-reggae, maracatu — meets electronic machines, loops and samples.

Trained in Brazil by master percussionists such as Orlando Costa and Arrepiado, he has shared the stage for more than twenty years with international artists including Seu Jorge, Paula Lima, Monica Passos, Chico César, Sages comme des Sauvages and Lucas Santtana. He is also the founder of the social project *Trash Re-percussion (Re-percutindo Lixo)*, which turns waste into percussion instruments with children from his home neighborhood.

With A SELVA, he delivers a physical, organic and danceable live show, somewhere between tropical ritual, club culture and contemporary trance. Built for world, organic-electro and Afro-Latin stages across Europe.

## Long version (≈ 320 words)

A *patamusicien* and Brazilian multi-percussionist originally from São Vicente, **Nyllo Canella** has been passionate about world percussion for more than twenty years. Constantly curious, he keeps exploring new sounds and new instruments — Indian tabla, Arabic derbake, tama, bata, cajon, djembe, and self-built instruments made from recycled objects such as PVC pipes, gas canisters, buckets and tires.

He started in Brazil with master percussionists **Orlando Costa** and **Arrepiado**, who taught him samba, afoxé, samba-reggae, maracatu and other Afro-Brazilian rhythms. His musical sensitivity quickly led him to share the stage with major international artists, including **Seu Jorge, Paula Lima, Monica Passos, Benito de Paula, Arlindo et Sombrinha, Chico César**, among others.

In 2012, he released his first album in Brazil with the group **Saramandaia**, to strong critical acclaim. The same year, he moved to Belgium and multiplied collaborations: **Tupynagroove, UTZ, O Polvo, Yallah Bye, Botafogo, Sages comme des Sauvages, Ivan Tirtiaux, Nu Jazz**, as well as studio work with **Lucas Santtana** (*Brasiliano*), **Manu Louis** (*Cream Parade*) and **Blue Bossa Liberté / Cecilia Peçanha**.

## A SELVA — live project

It is in Belgium that he created **A SELVA**, his own live project, pushing further the hybridization between Afro-Brazilian percussion and organic electronic music. On stage: a modular, instinctive setup where skins, woods, metals, voice, sound objects and organic pulses meet drum machines, loops, samples and club textures. The result: a physical, danceable Samba Tech, between tropical ritual, club culture and contemporary trance.

## Commitment

Back in Brazil, Nyllo Canella also founded the social project *Trash Re-percussion (Re-percutindo Lixo)*, which recycles waste into percussion instruments with children from working-class neighborhoods of São Vicente.

## In short

An explosive, hybrid and sincere live show, for world, organic-electro, Afro-Latin stages and festivals looking for a high-intensity, body-driven live experience.
