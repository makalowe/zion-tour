**Nyllo Canela** (born 1 November 1980 in São Vicente, São Paulo, Brazil) is a Brazilian multi-percussionist based in Belgium. He is best known as a founding member of **A SELVA**, a samba tech project based in Brussels.

> ⚠️ *Several claims in this article are not supported by reliable secondary sources and are marked with {{citation needed}}.*

## Biography

Canela was born and raised in São Vicente, on the coast of São Paulo State, where he began studying percussion with local masters from the Santos region. He continued his training at the São Paulo School of Music before relocating to Belgium in 2012.{{citation needed}}

## Career

### Early career in Brazil (2000s)

In Brazil, Canela worked as a live and studio percussionist, collaborating with several Brazilian artists including Seu Jorge, Paula Lima and Monica Passos, in concerts and recordings.{{citation needed}}

## A SELVA

**A SELVA** is Canela's current project, founded in Brussels and focused on samba tech — a fusion of traditional samba and electronic music. The group performs regularly across Belgium and France.{{citation needed}}

## Community work

Alongside his artistic career, Canela developed the **Trash Re-percussion** (*Re-percutindo Lixo*) project in São Vicente: an initiative that repurposes recovered materials into percussion instruments for educational and social purposes.{{citation needed}}

## Style and influences

Canela performs a wide range of Brazilian percussion (surdo, caixa, tamborim, repique) and African percussion (djembé, dundun). His approach blends Brazilian rhythmic tradition with contemporary sonic exploration.{{citation needed}}
